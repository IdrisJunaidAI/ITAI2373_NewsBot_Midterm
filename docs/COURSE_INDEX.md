## Evidence principles

* A notebook with saved outputs is evidence of a recorded execution, not proof of deployment reliability.
* A report is evidence of interpretation and documentation, not an independent test.
* A project link is evidence of a published repository, not necessarily a continuously available live service.
* Research metrics must always retain their source dataset, split, model and evaluation scope.
* The NewsBot final uses historically labeled BBC analytics, synthetic conversational demonstration content and current RSS links for different purposes. Those sources are not interchangeable.
