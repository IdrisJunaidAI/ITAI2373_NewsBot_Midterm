# Research integrity, attribution and governance

## Scope

This is a public educational NLP portfolio. It is not a regulator approved system, a medical device, a validated GxP workflow or a production source of verified news.

## Authorship and academic integrity

The midterm was originally framed as collaborative coursework. The individual report documents the student's verified contributions in data acquisition, environment recovery, Colab execution, result review, interpretation and documentation. Neither this portfolio nor the reports claim that every original project component was written independently. Instructor templates and contributions by other participants remain attributable to their respective authors.

AI tools were used for technical assistance, code scaffolding, troubleshooting and documentation. The student remains responsible for understanding, verifying and accurately describing outputs.

## Evidence levels

| Evidence level | Acceptable claim |
| --- | --- |
| Written reflection | Demonstrates reasoning and understanding |
| Notebook with saved outputs | Demonstrates a recorded execution with its recorded environment and inputs |
| Held out result | Measures performance on a specific dataset and split only |
| Automated tests | Demonstrates passing cases in the tested code version, not universal correctness |
| Deployed interface | Demonstrates user access if availability is independently checked |
| Validation for regulated use | Requires a distinct controlled validation and governance effort; not provided here |

## Prohibited inference

Do not interpret a classroom grade as a model accuracy figure. Do not conflate the midterm and final classification experiments. Do not treat entity cooccurrence as verified affiliation or sentiment score as a clinical judgment. Do not label controlled synthetic multilingual examples as real world multilingual news validation. Do not imply that current RSS headlines were part of the historical BBC corpus.

## Privacy and data rights

No credentials, keys, private company documents, personal grades, protected health information or peer discussion screenshots should be published. Links to the BBC dataset are not a license to redistribute full article text. All future datasets require a review of rights, confidentiality and representativeness.

## Intended applications

News routing and intelligence are proposed support functions. Any pharmaceutical quality, patient safety, regulatory or clinical implementation needs documented intended use, validated requirements, qualified data, auditability, human oversight and change control.

## Maintenance

Review external links, source provenance and metrics when updating. Preserve original evidence artifacts; add clearly labeled new experiments rather than silently rewriting past results.
