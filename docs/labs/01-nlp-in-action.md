# Introduction to NLP: Fiction as a requirements exercise

**Submission:** NLP in Action: From Pop Culture to Processes, August 2026.

The individual reflection compared **Rosie the Robot** from *The Jetsons* with **JARVIS** from *Iron Man*. The selected deeper example was Rosie because an assistant performing physical tasks must translate ordinary spoken language into context appropriate actions.

## Functional model

1. **Speech recognition:** Identify the words a person says despite accent, speed and background variation.
2. **Language understanding:** Interpret an intended request, even if indirect. Describing a messy room may imply a request for cleaning without stating a command.
3. **Context and decision:** Connect meaning to the situation and determine whether to respond verbally, ask for clarification or perform a physical task.
4. **Feedback:** Communicate the response or result appropriately.

**Core insight:** Word recognition alone cannot capture intent. A useful embodied system needs interpretation, contextual reasoning and the ability to request clarification when necessary.

This is conceptual analysis of fictional systems, not a claimed implementation of speech recognition or physical robotics. The original submission also included discussion participation evidence; classmate material is intentionally not republished here.
